Where It All Began 🌸

01
15 FEBRUARY 2026 · SUNDAY

The ISRO Training Day

It started with a one-day ISRO training. We were both in black, it was a long day, and there were only tiny moments of eye contact. Nothing looked like a big beginning at the time — but somehow, that day became the first page of our story.

“Sometimes a whole story begins with one small look.” ♡

“Can I Get a Charger?” 🔌

Our first conversation began with something as simple as asking for a charger. Later came the Instagram messages — good morning, good night, talking about college days, marks, exams, and all the ordinary things that slowly started feeling special.

OUR STORY

Every Little Chapter 💕

03

The Beginning of Our Friendship 💬

I started asking about the sad Instagram notes he posted. He replied in his own cute, nose-cut way. Then came more good mornings, good nights, questions about college, replies to notes, and those tiny conversations that slowly became part of our days.

I didn't know then that these ordinary messages would become some of my favourite memories.
04

The “For Whom?” Note Moment 👀

One day, he put an Instagram note and I asked, “For whom did you put the note?” Then he asked me the same question. I had also put a note and said I put it for someone to see. He simply said “ok.” I asked why he didn't ask who, and he replied that it was my personal matter. I got a little upset and told him he could ask me anything. His “ohhh like that” reply became one of those tiny moments I still remember.

05

The Crush That Became Something More 🦋

I already had a crush on him from first year, first semester. After the ISRO training in second semester, I started liking him even more. I would blush, smile like crazy, and feel butterflies whenever I saw him. It was such a beautiful feeling.

06
03 MARCH 2026

The Day We Said It 💌

By then our normal conversations had slowly grown into something more. When I heard that a girl from his class liked him, I became scared and wondered what would happen if someone proposed to him. So I asked him about it on Instagram. He said, “Let's see when it comes.” That made me even more nervous.

Finally I told him that I wanted to ask something — that I liked him, but didn't know whether he liked me too. Then came the words I had been hoping for: he told me he loved me too. When I asked what he meant, he made it clear — he loved me too. 🌹

For a few minutes, I couldn't believe I was actually committed to Vignesh. We both felt happy, emotional, and completely surprised by how beautifully that moment turned out.

03.03.2026 — The beginning of our love story. ♡
07
27 MARCH 2026

Our First Cheek Kiss 🌷

On college day, after the morning competition, we ate fried rice together. He fed me, then gave me a cheek kiss — and I kissed his cheek too. A simple little moment that became one of our most special dates.

08

The Silly Fights 😂💗

Sometimes I start silly little fights for silly reasons. Somehow, instead of staying angry, they turn into cute conversations, understanding, and making up. Even our small disagreements became part of learning each other better.

09

The Moments That Made Me Feel Cared For 🥹

The moments when he worried about me, stood up for me, cared for me when I was upset, and treated me with so much gentleness are some of the memories I treasure most. Those little acts of care made the relationship feel safe, warm, and meaningful.
